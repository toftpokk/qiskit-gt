# Non-AI Ideas:
1. Quantum Repeater Entanglement Puzzle: Game

quantum repeaters need to swap and generate new pairs in a particular order. Why not turn that into a game? Swap right and you get points.

variations: larger networks, more complex networks, more users
more mechanics: fidelity, a timer

Ref: http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02575

2. Quantum AntiChamber: An interactive experience

You have a "measurement" device, go through rooms and explore the quantum museum in an interactive way.

Ref: http://www.antichamber-game.com/
